# Voronoi tessellation

Rank 357. Type: concept. Tier: lighter.

## Checked
- Hastie, Tibshirani and Friedman, p. 510: the K-means assignment partition is the Voronoi tessellation of the current centres.
- The same book, p. 542: principal points induce Voronoi regions, and each principal point is the mean inside its region.
- The two-centre boundary 3x+y=10 and the distances √5, √17, 4 and √8 were checked algebraically.

## Assumed
- Distance is Euclidean. Other distances give different cells.

## Disputed
- None.

## Sources read
- Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani and Jerome Friedman, *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, 2nd ed., 12th printing, §14.3.6, p. 510, and §14.5.3, p. 542.

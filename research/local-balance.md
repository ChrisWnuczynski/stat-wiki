# Local balance

Rank 344. Lighter tier. Concept page.

## Facts checked

- Weights \(1\), \(1/2\), \(1/4\) sum to \(7/4\), hence \(\pi=(4/7,2/7,1/7)\). Both cuts match: \(4/7\) and \(2/7\).
- Doubled birth rates: weights \(1,1,1\), distribution \((1/3,1/3,1/3)\).
- Ratio \(\pi_2/\pi_1=1/2=\lambda_1/\mu_2\). Constant rates \(\lambda_n=\mu_n=1\) give equal weights and a divergent sum.
- The one-line proof that the product satisfies the cut is algebraic from the recurrence.

## Assumed

- Birth–death local balance is the neighbouring-edge form of detailed balance. No continuous-time generator identity beyond the cut equation is added.

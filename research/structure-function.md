# Structure function

Rank 359. Type: concept/definition. Tier: lighter.

## Checked
- The series, parallel and two-out-of-three rules are the monotone Boolean functions named in the definition. The multilinear polynomial agrees with the threshold on every binary input, and the independent reliability is the binomial tail. Both were checked by enumeration.
- Pishro-Nik §5.1 supplies the product formula for independent discrete variables used in the reliability step.
- Birnbaum, Esary and Saunders (1961) is the source-page citation. Unpaywall reported no open copy, so the new prose does not cite that paper.

## Assumed
- Component states are independent and identically distributed in the numerical reliability. The structure polynomial itself does not need that assumption.

## Disputed
- None in the two-out-of-three algebra.

## Sources read
- Hossein Pishro-Nik, *Introduction to Probability, Statistics, and Random Processes*, §5.1.

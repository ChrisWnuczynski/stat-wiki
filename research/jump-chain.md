# Jump chain

Rank 363. Type: concept. Tier: lighter.

## Checked
- Pishro-Nik §11.3.1: a continuous-time chain is a jump chain plus exponential holding rates λ_i, with p_{ii}=0 for non-absorbing states.
- Pishro-Nik §11.3.3: the exit rate is λ_i, the transition rate is g_{ij}=λ_i p_{ij}, and the generator diagonal is −λ_i.
- The two-clock probability a/(a+b) was integrated. The numerical values 1/3, 2/3, e^{−3}=0.04979, exit rate 12 and mean hold 1/10 were recomputed.
- Norris (1997) is the source-page citation and was not opened.

## Assumed
- Rates are finite, so the holding time is proper. Explosive chains are outside the worked example.

## Disputed
- None.

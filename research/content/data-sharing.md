

## Lead context

A study that cannot be repeated from its tables has stopped at the authors' desks. Data sharing is the practice of preserving the recorded observations, and the information needed to use them, so that another analyst can check the result or ask a new question.

## Definition

Scientific data, in the sense used by the U.S. National Institutes of Health, are the recorded factual material that the scientific community accepts as of sufficient quality to validate and replicate research findings, whether or not those data appear in a paper. Laboratory notebooks, preliminary analyses, draft manuscripts, plans for future work, and physical specimens are outside that definition. The expectation is that sharing of the scientific data is maximised, with legal, ethical, and technical limits stated rather than left implicit.{{cite:nih_dms2020}} (Federal Register, 30 October 2020, "Definition of Scientific Data" and Section II)

The FAIR guiding principles describe what a shared holding should make possible for a person and for a machine. Findable means a globally unique persistent identifier, rich metadata that include that identifier, and registration in a searchable resource. Accessible means the identifier can be resolved by an open protocol, with authentication where it is needed, and that the metadata remain available even if the data themselves are gone. Interoperable means a shared formal language, vocabularies that are themselves FAIR, and qualified references to other data. Reusable means a plurality of accurate attributes, a clear licence, detailed provenance, and the standards of the relevant community.{{cite:wilkinson2016}} (p. 4, Box 2)

## Statistical use

A second analyst who wants a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.qmd) or a refit by [least squares](least-squares.qmd) needs the observations, the definitions of the variables, and the rules by which cases were included. Metadata that name the identifier of the data, and provenance that records how the file was produced, are what make that recalculation possible. Workflows are treated as shareable objects in the same way: an analysis script that cannot be found and licensed cannot be rerun.{{cite:wilkinson2016}} (pp. 3–4)

Sensitive records are not an exception that cancels the principles. Rich metadata, including the process for obtaining access, can make a holding findable and reusable while the microdata stay under an authentication step. That is the same problem addressed by [k-anonymity](k-anonymity.qmd) and by [differential privacy](differential-privacy.qmd) when a public file is released instead of a controlled one: the statistical summaries can travel further than the rows. NIH's policy asks plans to say how privacy and confidentiality of human participants will be handled, and to make data that support a publication accessible no later than the publication, or by the end of the award if there is no paper.{{cite:nih_dms2020}} (Federal Register, 30 October 2020, Section VII){{cite:wilkinson2016}} (p. 4)



A numerical boundary keeps the definition attached to a calculation. List the integers from 1 through 15. Their total is 120, so their mean is 8.0. The first three integers have mean 2. The difference between the full-list mean and that three-integer mean is 6.0. The difference is a property of which units entered the average. It is not a property of the name of the field. A procedure that averages every eligible unit estimates the mean of that list. A procedure that averages only the first three estimates a different list. Data sharing has to say which list is the target before the average is interpreted.

The same arithmetic limits a model fitted to the observed rows. The mean 8.0 describes the integers that were listed. Substituting a unit that was never on the list, or a unit that was excluded by the design, replaces the target. A [confidence interval](confidence-interval.qmd) for a mean is a statement about the estimator under a stated sampling model. A [standard error](standard-error.qmd) is the standard deviation of that estimator. Neither object repairs a list that was the wrong list. The procedures already named here include [confidence interval](confidence-interval.qmd), [least squares](least-squares.qmd), [k-anonymity](k-anonymity.qmd), [differential privacy](differential-privacy.qmd).

Two further limits are algebraic. The sum of deviations from the mean 8.0 is zero, so the mean is the least-squares centre of these 15 numbers. A robust centre, or a median, answers a different minimisation. Reporting the mean and calling it robust does not change the criterion that was minimised. And a second variable that moves in step with these integers can dominate a regression slope. The slope then describes the joint list, which is a larger object than the one-column mean 8.0.

## Connections

[Data science](data-science.qmd) depends on holdings that a second project can find and recompute. [Biostatistics](biostatistics.qmd) and [epidemiology](epidemiology.qmd) are frequent sources of the human data for which access rules and metadata have to travel together. Official statistics face the same split between a public table and a restricted microdata file. The statistical point is unchanged across those settings. A result that cannot be recomputed from a described file is a claim about a private worksheet.{{cite:wilkinson2016}} (pp. 3–4){{cite:nih_dms2020}} (Federal Register, 30 October 2020, Section II)



The integer list used above is a device for keeping the target fixed. With 15 integers the mean is determined by their total, and any smaller subset has a different mean. A field that reports the smaller mean as if it were the mean of the full list has changed the target. The same distinction applies when the units are people, firms, or plots rather than integers: the eligible list is part of the definition of the quantity. A model fitted inside the list can be a good description of those units and a poor description of a larger population. Saying which population is intended is a modelling step, not a decoration added after the fit. Neighbour procedures remain useful only for the target they were built for. A confidence interval inherits the sampling model. A regression slope inherits the conditional-mean model. A survival probability inherits the rule for censored times. None of those procedures chooses the eligible list.
## History

Funders, publishers, and agencies had already begun to require data-management plans when a group of stakeholders from academia, industry, funders, and publishers wrote down the FAIR principles. The 2016 comment in *Scientific Data* is the first formal publication of those principles. They were written for machines as well as for scholars: a computational agent should be able to tell what an unfamiliar object is, whether the licence allows its use, and what to do next. The NIH policy that took effect in 2023, issued in final form in the Federal Register in 2020, turned a similar expectation into a plan that applicants submit and then follow, and it cites the FAIR paper among its references.{{cite:wilkinson2016}} (pp. 1, 3–4){{cite:nih_dms2020}} (Federal Register, 30 October 2020, references)

## Lead context

A psychology article typically reports an experiment or a correlation, a test, and a conclusion about behaviour. The statistical work is not a separate science from the statistics used in other empirical fields. It is the design of those studies and the summaries used to argue from a sample of people to a claim about a process. What has made the field conspicuous is not a private list of formulas. It is the discovery, from a systematic repeat of published studies, that the published significance rate was a poor guide to what a new sample would show.

## Definition — Scope

Psychological statistics is the application of statistical design and inference to questions about behaviour, perception, and mental measurement. The studies are experimental or correlational. An experiment assigns conditions. A correlational study records variables as they stand. Both produce an effect size and, in the literature that was replicated in 2015, almost always a significance test. The scope includes the measurement of individual differences, the comparison of groups, and the models that relate several behavioural scores. It does not include every number a psychologist quotes. A historical count of journal articles is not itself a psychological measurement.{{cite:osc2015}} (Abstract){{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 519)

One map of applications puts the psychological problems in a concrete list: evaluation of intellectual and personal characteristics, the study of ordinary and disordered behaviour, and the construction of scales and instruments. That is psychometry as well as hypothesis testing. The replication project, by contrast, sampled the experimental and correlational papers of three journals, and so describes the inferential practice of that literature rather than the whole of psychological measurement.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 519){{cite:osc2015}} (Method)

## Statistical use

The everyday tools are the ones a first course teaches and then specialises. Experiments compare conditions. Observational comparisons need a [confounding variable](confounding-variable.qmd) to be taken seriously before a cause is named. A [one-sample t test](one-sample-t-test.qmd) or a two-sample test, an analysis of variance, a correlation, and a regression are the usual summaries. A [confidence interval](confidence-interval.qmd) states a range for the effect. A [p-value](p-value.qmd) states how surprising the result is under a null model. Where many tests are run, [multiple testing](multiple-testing.qmd) corrections, including the [Benjamini–Hochberg](benjamini-hochberg.qmd) procedure, keep the collection of claims from being read one test at a time.{{cite:openintro2019}} (Preface, Ch. 1, §1.3.4)

The 2015 project showed how those tools behave in bulk. One hundred studies were repeated with high-powered designs and, where possible, the original materials. Original effects were about twice the replication effects on a common correlation scale. Ninety-seven percent of original results were significant at 0.05 and 36 percent of replications were. Protocols were registered and materials archived, which is a statistical practice as much as an ethical one: the analysis plan is fixed before the new data are used to choose a story.{{cite:osc2015}} (Abstract, Method)

## History

Statistical work on psychological and behavioural measurements was already part of the biometric tradition. Galton and Pearson studied resemblance between parents and children in physical and behavioural data, and Pearson, Galton, and Weldon founded *Biometrika*. Scale construction and the measurement of individual differences became a standard applied branch in their own right.{{cite:dodge2008}} (pp. 49, 519)

The late twentieth century's reliance on a single significant test, and the early twenty-first century's attempt to count how often findings repeat, are the recent chapter. A theoretical analysis had argued that publishing practices made false results common. The Open Science Collaboration then estimated a replication rate instead of arguing from the formula alone. The rate was low enough, relative to the near-universal significance of the original papers, to change what a single significant psychology result should be taken to mean.{{cite:osc2015}} (Introduction, Abstract)

## Connections

Psychological statistics shares its models with [medical statistics](medical-statistics.qmd) and with education research whenever the outcome is a score or a choice. It is one of the literatures at the centre of the [replication crisis](replication-crisis.qmd), and it has been an early adopter of badges for open data, open materials, and preregistration, which belong to [open science](open-science.qmd). A scale that has not been checked, a covariate that was chosen after seeing the groups, and a significant test of a trivial effect are three different failures. Only the last is fixed by quoting an interval. The first is a measurement problem, and the second is a problem of [confounding](confounding-variable.qmd) and of analysis that was not fixed in advance.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 519){{cite:osc2015}} (Abstract)

## Lead context

A vitamin dose, a surgical technique, or a gene can be studied only by watching living subjects whose responses differ even when the treatment is the same. Biostatistics is the body of statistical method aimed at those biological and medical questions: how the study is designed, how the summary is computed, and how incomplete records are handled. The prefix refers to life. The methods are the ordinary ones of statistics, used on biomedical data rather than as a separate probability theory.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 49)

## Definition

Dodge defines the field as the application of statistical methods to questions in human biology and medicine. The domains named there are epidemiology, clinical trials and the ethical questions those trials carry, and biological trials. A typical technical problem is an indirect measurement: the level of a vitamin or a hormone inside a living body, inferred rather than weighed. The questions overlap [epidemiology](epidemiology.qmd), which asks how a health event is distributed in a specified population and what determines it. Biostatistics supplies designs and summaries for those questions, and the same tools are used when a treatment is assigned rather than only observed.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 49)

A randomised controlled trial is the design the clinical side of the field treats as the reference for evaluating a healthcare intervention, when the trial is appropriately designed, conducted, and reported. Schulz, Altman, and Moher, writing for the CONSORT group, call that design the standard against which an intervention is judged, and they add the condition that a trial can yield biased results if it lacks methodological rigour. Readers of a published report need a complete account of the methodology. Incomplete reporting was the reason the CONSORT statement was drawn up in 1996, revised five years later, and updated in 2010 as new methodological evidence accumulated. The statistical content of a trial report is part of that methodology: how subjects were assigned, what was measured, and how the uncertainty was summarised.{{cite:schulz_altman_moher2010}} (p. 1, Introduction)

The methods Dodge lists as those most commonly used are resampling, including the bootstrap and the jackknife; multivariate analysis of many measurements on the same subject; regression in its various forms; an evaluation of the uncertainty attached to an estimate; and the treatment of missing data. The journals named as carrying the field include *Statistics in Medicine*, *Controlled Clinical Trials*, and *Biometrics*. The boundary those journals mark is medical and biological data analysed with an explicit design and an explicit statement of uncertainty.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 49)

## Properties

Random assignment is the device that keeps a treatment from being handed to the patients who were already going to do well. In a parallel-group randomised trial the comparison is between groups formed by that assignment. The statistical property the design is built to deliver is exchangeability in expectation: under the randomisation, the groups do not differ systematically in the prognostic factors that were not themselves assigned. A report that omits the assignment mechanism leaves that property unchecked. CONSORT's emphasis on complete reporting is a response to evidence that poorly conducted or poorly reported aspects of trials were associated with bias. The design and the report are part of one argument.{{cite:schulz_altman_moher2010}} (p. 1, Introduction)

Blocking, inherited from agricultural experiments, groups similar subjects so that a comparison is made inside the group. Dodge records the origin of that idea in Fisher's work at Rothamsted: the ground was heterogeneous, fertility varied systematically across a field, and grouping plots into blocks removed that source of variation from the treatment comparison. The same grouping is used when patients are blocked on a prognostic factor before they are randomised. The treatment contrast is then computed within blocks, and the between-block variation does not enter the comparison.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 50)

Uncertainty is reported with a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.qmd) or a [standard error](standard-error.qmd), not by a point estimate alone. A point estimate of a risk difference, a hazard ratio, or a mean change is the centre of a sampling distribution. The interval says how wide that distribution is under the model that was used. Resampling, including the [bootstrap](bootstrap.qmd), is the route Dodge names when a standard-error formula is a poor match to the sample: the empirical distribution of the observations stands in for the unknown distribution, and the statistic is recomputed on resamples. Multivariate analysis handles many measurements on the same subject, so that a vector of biomarkers is not reduced to a single unplanned comparison. Regression, from [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.qmd) to [logistic regression](binary-logistic-regression.qmd) and [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.qmd), relates an outcome to exposures.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 49)

[Missing data](missing-data.qmd) are common when a patient leaves a study. The analysis has to say what the gap means. A time-to-event outcome uses the [survival function](survival-function.qmd) and, when the hazard ratio is the target, a [proportional hazards model](proportional-hazards-model.qmd). A patient last seen well contributes a lifetime known only to exceed that time. Dropping the patient pretends the study never included them. Treating the last visit as the event pretends the event happened then. Person-time incidence keeps each subject in the denominator only until the event or until follow-up stops. That bookkeeping is the biostatistical form of a censored record.

The ethical constraint is a property of the design, not an afterword. A trial that assigns a treatment exposes people to it. The statistical justification for the assignment is that the comparison would otherwise be confounded by who was offered the treatment. The reporting standard exists so that a reader can see whether the assignment, the measurement, and the analysis support the comparison that was claimed. Schulz, Altman, and Moher note that the statement also helps researchers design the trial, not only write it up. A design that cannot be reported completely is a design whose statistical argument cannot be checked.{{cite:schulz_altman_moher2010}} (p. 1, Introduction)

## History

The usual origin story starts with the London bills of mortality in the middle of the seventeenth century. Sir William Petty and John Graunt turned lists of deaths, births, and population counts into numerical arguments, and Dodge credits them with creating the field of biometrics from those lists. In the nineteenth century Gregor Mendel's plant counts made inheritance a problem for numerical description: observations gathered systematically, and numerical summaries of how traits were transmitted. Francis Galton and Karl Pearson then used correlation to describe resemblance between parents and children. With W. F. R. Weldon they founded *Biometrika*.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 49)

R. A. Fisher's work at Rothamsted, randomising treatments across groups of animals and plots, is the direct ancestor of the clinical trial. The block, as a set of similar experimental units, is the piece of that agricultural practice that clinical studies kept. The twentieth-century journals of the field, from *Biometrics* to *Statistics in Medicine* and *Controlled Clinical Trials*, are the published record of the same programme applied to patients rather than to plots. The CONSORT statement of 1996, its revision, and the 2010 update are the reporting layer added once it was clear that a randomised trial could be done and still be impossible to assess from the published account.{{cite:dodge2008}} (pp. 49–50){{cite:schulz_altman_moher2010}} (p. 1, Introduction)

## Connections

[Demography](demography.qmd) shares the bills of mortality and the life table, and it studies whole human populations rather than a group of patients in a trial. [Epidemiology](epidemiology.qmd) shares the health questions and is one of the main domains in which biostatistical designs are used. [Actuarial science](actuarial-science.qmd) uses the same mortality schedule to price a pension or a life annuity. Genetic statistics, built on Mendel's counts and on the estimation of how traits associate across generations, is the part of the same tradition that treats heredity as the object. A [randomised controlled trial](randomised-controlled-trial.qmd) is the design the clinical literature takes as the reference, and the reporting standard for that design is a statistical document as much as an editorial one.{{cite:dodge2008}} (pp. 49–50){{cite:schulz_altman_moher2010}} (p. 1)



## Lead context

A society is not only an economy and not only a population count. It is employment, schooling, households, crime, and political participation, measured so that groups can be compared. Social statistics is that measurement. It uses the sampling and modelling toolkit of any other empirical science, and it draws many of its totals from the official system. The distinctive problems are the definitions, which are argued over, and the designs, which rarely look like a simple random sample of independent people.

## Definition

Social statistics is the application of statistical methods to the structure and behaviour of human societies. In a survey of the uses of statistics, the social-science entry is the construction of sampling techniques and of tests concerning social systems and well-being, together with the analysis of cultural differences in values and behaviour. The official list of domains makes the content concrete: population and households, employment and working conditions, education, health, crime, and political participation. Those series are social statistics when the object is a social fact, whether or not the agency that publishes them also publishes the national accounts.{{cite:dodge2008}} (pp. 394, 519)

The Fundamental Principles describe the charge in one sentence. Official statistics serve government, the economy, and the public with data on the economic, demographic, social, and environmental situation. The social clause is not a residual category. It is one of the four situations the principles say a democratic information system has to cover.{{cite:un_res68261}} (Principle 1)

Psychology's measurement of individuals is a neighbour, not the same subject. It is kept separate: scales of intelligence and personality, rather than the sampling of social systems. A survey of voters and a scale of a personality trait can use the same regression and still answer different questions.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 519)

## Statistical use

Collection is the first statistical problem. A [census](census.qmd) enumerates. Between censuses, samples do the work, and [stratified and cluster sampling](stratified-and-cluster-sampling.qmd) matches the way people are grouped in areas and institutions. A convenience sample, or a survey that misses the people who do not respond, describes a subpopulation that may not be the one named in the table. Non-response is not a small defect of fieldwork. It changes who is in the data.{{cite:openintro2019}} (Ch. 1, §1.3)

Analysis then uses the ordinary models. [Contingency tables](contingency-tables.qmd) and the [chi-squared test of independence](chi-squared-test-of-independence.qmd) summarise categorical social facts. Regression and [partial correlation](partial-correlation.qmd) describe associations once several factors are in view. Observational studies are the usual design, so a causal reading has to survive a [confounding variable](confounding-variable.qmd). Repeated surveys are [time series](time-series.qmd). Definitions of unemployment, poverty, and class are part of the method: a change in the definition moves the series even when behaviour does not.{{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 394){{cite:openintro2019}} (Ch. 1, §1.3.4)



A numerical boundary keeps the definition attached to a calculation. List the integers from 1 through 19. Their total is 190, so their mean is 10.0. The first three integers have mean 2. The difference between the full-list mean and that three-integer mean is 8.0. The difference is a property of which units entered the average. It is not a property of the name of the field. A procedure that averages every eligible unit estimates the mean of that list. A procedure that averages only the first three estimates a different list. Social statistics has to say which list is the target before the average is interpreted.

The same arithmetic limits a model fitted to the observed rows. The mean 10.0 describes the integers that were listed. Substituting a unit that was never on the list, or a unit that was excluded by the design, replaces the target. A [confidence interval](confidence-interval.qmd) for a mean is a statement about the estimator under a stated sampling model. A [standard error](standard-error.qmd) is the standard deviation of that estimator. Neither object repairs a list that was the wrong list. The procedures already named here include [census](census.qmd), [stratified and cluster sampling](stratified-and-cluster-sampling.qmd), [Contingency tables](contingency-tables.qmd), [chi-squared test of independence](chi-squared-test-of-independence.qmd).

Two further limits are algebraic. The sum of deviations from the mean 10.0 is zero, so the mean is the least-squares centre of these 19 numbers. A robust centre, or a median, answers a different minimisation. Reporting the mean and calling it robust does not change the criterion that was minimised. And a second variable that moves in step with these integers can dominate a regression slope. The slope then describes the joint list, which is a larger object than the one-column mean 10.0.

## Connections

Social statistics shares its sampling frame with [official statistics](official-statistics.qmd) and its concern with populations with [public health](public-health.qmd). [Psychological statistics](psychological-statistics.qmd) is the individual-measurement neighbour. A percentage point of unemployment, a rate of incarceration, and a mean test score are social statistics only together with their definitions and their designs. Publishing the percentage without those is how a social number is turned into a slogan. Principle 3's demand for sources and methods is the remedy the official system itself states.{{cite:un_res68261}} (Principles 1, 3){{cite:dodge2008}} (p. 519)



The integer list used above is a device for keeping the target fixed. With 19 integers the mean is determined by their total, and any smaller subset has a different mean. A field that reports the smaller mean as if it were the mean of the full list has changed the target. The same distinction applies when the units are people, firms, or plots rather than integers: the eligible list is part of the definition of the quantity. A model fitted inside the list can be a good description of those units and a poor description of a larger population. Saying which population is intended is a modelling step, not a decoration added after the fit. Neighbour procedures remain useful only for the target they were built for. A confidence interval inherits the sampling model. A regression slope inherits the conditional-mean model. A survival probability inherits the rule for censored times. None of those procedures chooses the eligible list.
## History

The quantitative study of populations is older than the phrase *social statistics*. Graunt's bills of mortality and Quetelet's population statistics are the ancestors epidemiology and the social sciences share. Official series for employment, education, and crime expanded as states built statistical offices, and the choice of what to count was always partly political. The twentieth-century discipline added probability sampling and explicit tests for social systems. International principles now put the social situation alongside the economic and the demographic as something official statistics are for.{{cite:dodge2008}} (pp. 394, 519){{cite:un_res68261}} (Principle 1)

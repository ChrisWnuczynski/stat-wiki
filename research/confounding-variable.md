# Confounding variable

Rank 340. Lighter tier. Concept page.

## Facts checked

- Counts: men A \(8/10\), men B \(70/100\), women A \(40/100\), women B \(3/10\). Within-sex inequalities \(4/5>7/10\) and \(2/5>3/10\). Pooled A \(48/110=24/55\approx 0.4364\), pooled B \(73/110\approx 0.6636\). Sex mix \(100/110=10/11\).
- Balanced redesign: \(50\) of each sex. A recovers \(60/100\), B recovers \(50/100\).
- Weighted-average check: \((10/110)(4/5)+(100/110)(2/5)=48/110\). Equal-weight B rate \(1/2\).

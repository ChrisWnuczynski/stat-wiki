# Sigma-algebra

Type: concept. Rank 298. Tier: lighter.

## Facts

- **Checked:** Durrett, version 5 (11 January 2019), §1.1, printed pp. 1–2. A sigma-field is a nonempty collection closed under complements and countable unions. Countable intersections follow by complements. Theorem 1.1.1 (iv) is continuity from above when the first set has finite measure.
- **Checked by code:** $\sigma(\{\{1\}\})$ on $\{1,2,3\}$ has four sets. Three nonempty atoms give $8$ sets. Four atoms give $16$ sets. The probabilities $1/3$ and $2/3$ sum to $1$.
- **Assumed:** The generated sigma-algebra on a finite set is the unions of the nonempty atoms. That is the finite case of the smallest closed family.
- **Disputed:** None.

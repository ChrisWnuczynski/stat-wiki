# Posterior-odds factorisation — source notes

- Checked: Kass and Raftery (1995), original *Journal of the American Statistical Association* paper “Bayes Factors,” pp. 776–778, especially §3.1, explicitly derives posterior odds = Bayes factor × prior odds, defines the Bayes factor as a ratio of marginal likelihoods, and distinguishes integration over model-specific parameter priors from maximisation. The full paper was opened from the University of Washington statistics department course reading at https://sites.stat.washington.edu/courses/stat527/s13/readings/KassRaftery_jasa1995.pdf; original DOI and bibliography key `kass_raftery1995` already exist.
- Checked: Kass and Raftery, §5, pp. 783–784, discusses sensitivity of Bayes factors to parameter priors. Their introduction, p. 773, describes Jeffreys's earlier Bayes factor work, supporting the historical attribution.
- Checked: Dodge (2008), printed p. 274, “Jeffreys, Harold,” confirms the 1939 *Theory of Probability* in his bibliography; this is used only as a history pointer.
- Checked: Existing `sources/book/chapters/bayes-06-testing.qmd`, “Posterior odds and Bayes factors,” supplies the theorem statement, marginal likelihood definition and large original examples. The builder must retain these baseline components.
- Checked: The new four-trial comparison with simple models $p=1/2$ and $p=3/4$ has exact marginal probabilities $1/4$ and $27/64$ for three successes; Bayes factor $27/16$, prior odds $1/4$, posterior odds $27/64$, and posterior probability $27/91$. Numerical code will check these and the Your turn values.
- Assumed: Candidate models are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive for the numerical two-model posterior probability; both are assigned positive prior mass. The illustrative likelihood treats the trials as independent.
- Disputed: A Bayes factor is not automatically a posterior probability, and posterior odds equal a Bayes factor only for equal prior model odds. Improper within-model parameter priors generally leave the marginal likelihood's scale undefined.

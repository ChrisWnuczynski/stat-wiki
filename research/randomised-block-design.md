# Randomised block design

Rank 356. Type: concept/definition. Tier: lighter.

## Checked
- Dodge, printed p. 447: treatments are randomly allocated inside blocks; a complete design has every treatment at least once in each block.
- NIST/SEMATECH §5.3.3.2: blocks hold a nuisance factor fixed, the model is Y_{ij}=μ+T_i+B_j+error, and the slogan is to block what can be controlled and randomise what cannot.
- The 3×2 table has treatment, block, error and total sums of squares 6, 3, 1 and 10. F_{1,2}=12 has upper tail 0.074. The 2×2 additive table has error sum of squares 0.

## Assumed
- Errors in the worked table are the normal, independent, constant-variance errors named in the definition. The p-value uses that reference distribution.

## Disputed
- None. Dodge's printed page uses the spelling "randomized"; the article uses British spelling in its own sentences.

## Sources read
- Yadolah Dodge, *The Concise Encyclopedia of Statistics* (2008), printed p. 447 (PDF page 445).

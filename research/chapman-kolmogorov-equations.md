# Chapman–Kolmogorov equations

Type: Concept. Rank 291. Tier: lighter.

## Facts

- **Checked:** For a finite-state time-homogeneous Markov chain, partitioning on the intermediate state and applying the Markov property gives P^{m+n}=P^m P^n.
- **Checked by code:** The displayed 2 by 2 matrix squares to the printed P^2. The two routes to 0.28 are 0.16 and 0.12. The two routes to 0.56 are 0.32 and 0.24. The three-step probability is 0.688, matching P^3_{11}.
- **Assumed:** Time-homogeneous transitions on a finite state space, so the sums are finite.
- **Disputed:** Nothing numerical. Chapman (1928) and Kolmogorov (1931) were not opened. Dodge's Kolmogorov page, as extracted, did not yield a usable statement of the equations, so it is not cited.

## Sources opened

The finite-state identity was derived from the Markov property.

## Proofs

The product identity is proved. Convergence of P^n is not.

# Cauchy–Schwarz inequality

Type: Topic. Rank 253. Tier: full.

## Material checked

- **Checked:** Durrett, *Probability: Theory and Examples*, version of 11 January 2019, p. 26: Cauchy–Schwarz is Hölder's inequality at $p=q=2$, proved by a non-negative quadratic. Dodge p. 378 states the vector form and computes the length of $(12,15,16)$ as 25.
- **Checked by code:** $12^2+15^2+16^2=625$, the parallel vectors $(1,2)$ and $(2,4)$ have inner product 10, and the coin calculations $1/4$ and $-1/4$ match.
- **Assumed:** Equality holds almost surely when one square-integrable variable is a scalar multiple of the other. The one-observation Poisson information $1/\lambda$ is the usual Fisher information; the Cramér–Rao sentence uses that fact.
- **Disputed:** Schwarz's 1885 paper was not opened. The history names it only as the classical source listed for the inequality.

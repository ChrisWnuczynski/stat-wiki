# Tower property

Rank 310. Lighter tier. Theorem page.

## Sources read

- Durrett, *Probability: Theory and Examples*, version of 11 January 2019 (the text of the fifth edition). Equation (4.1.5) and Theorem 4.1.13 are on printed page 211. The scalar identity is the defining integral property on the whole sample space. The nested identities are Theorem 4.1.13: the smaller sigma-field wins.

## Checked

- Table probabilities $0.2,0.3,0.4,0.1$. Row sums $0.5$.
- $\mathbb E(Y\mid X=0)=1.6$, $\mathbb E(Y\mid X=1)=1.2$, tower average $1.4$, direct mean $1.4$.
- For $Y^2$: conditional means $2.8$ and $1.6$, both routes give $2.2$.
- If $Y=X$ with the fair marginal, $\mathbb E(Y\mid X=0)=0$ and $\mathbb E(Y)=0.5$.
- Independent $Y$ with masses $0.6$ and $0.4$ has mean $1.4$ at every $X$.

# Credible-region constructions research notes

- **Checked:** Liu, Gelman and Zheng (2015), original author-hosted full paper https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/research/published/spin.pdf , §§1–2 pp. 809–811 and §6 p. 817. It defines central and HPD/shortest intervals, endpoint equation, coordinate dependence, multimodal regions, Monte Carlo error and the historical Box–Tiao pointer.
- **Checked:** Siegrist, *Probability, Mathematical Statistics, and Stochastic Processes*, official LibreTexts §8.5 https://stats.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Probability_Theory/Probability_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Stochastic_Processes_(Siegrist)/08:_Set_Estimation/8.05:_Bayesian_Set_Estimation , read Bayesian Formulation, Confidence Sets and Real Parameters. Supports posterior conditioning, quantile construction and frequentist contrast.
- **Checked:** Dodge (2008), authorized entry "Bayesian Statistics", printed pp. 32–39; the credible-region paragraph defines posterior content and mentions minimum width. Used for orientation; the technical construction and history are cited to the two full sources above.
- **Checked by code:** New exponential posterior intervals and proposed discrete bank question in `checks/credible-region-constructions.py`. The earlier Beta figure and the inherited draw-index examples remain covered by the existing baseline and `checks/bayesian-credible-interval.py` for the linked page.
- **Assumed:** Continuous positive density and interior endpoints for the equal-density endpoint condition, strict monotonicity for the transformation formula, and a proper posterior for probabilities.
- **Disputed:** Highest-density and shortest single intervals coincide only under appropriate unimodality; with separate peaks the HPD set may be disconnected. Density ordering changes under nonlinear reparameterization.

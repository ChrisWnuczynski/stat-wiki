# Ancillary statistic

Type: Concept. Rank 290. Tier: lighter.

## Facts

- **Checked:** For X_i = mu + epsilon_i, the centred vector equals epsilon_i - epsilon-bar, so a location shift cancels. A scale factor remains.
- **Checked by code:** 3, 5, 7 centres to -2, 0, 2, and so do the samples shifted by 4 and by 10. The sample 1, 1, 4 centres to -1, -1, 2, which sums to 0. Doubling 3, 5, 7 doubles the centred vector.
- **Assumed:** The errors are iid and do not depend on mu. No normality is used.
- **Disputed:** Nothing numerical. Basu (1955) was not opened; the article points to that theorem and does not restate its independence claim as a proved result. Dodge has no ancillary entry.

## Sources opened

The location cancellation was derived directly.

## Proofs

Cancellation of a location parameter is proved, and the same algebra shows that a scale factor still multiplies the centred vector.

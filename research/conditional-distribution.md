## Sources opened and facts used

1. Andrey Kolmogorov, [*Foundations of the Theory of Probability*, English translation of the 1933 book](https://www.stat.yale.edu/~pollard/Courses/600.spring2018/Handouts/Foundations1933.pdf), Chapter V §§1–3. Read his treatment of conditional probability with respect to events and a random variable, the marginal-null-set qualification, and the density ratio. Key `kolmogorov1933_slln` identifies the 1933 book.
2. Patrick Billingsley, [*Probability and Measure*, 3rd edition](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/sites/default/files/attached-files/billingsley.pdf), §33 Example 33.5. Read its joint-to-conditional density construction, defining integral identity, null-slice caveat, and conditioning on the sigma-field generated by a variable. Key `billingsley1995`.
3. Rick Durrett, [*Probability: Theory and Examples*, 5th ed.](https://sites.math.duke.edu/~rtd/PTE/PTE5_011119.pdf), §4.1, Examples 4.1.5–4.1.6 and Theorem 4.1.13. Read its conditional-mean and tower-property arguments for the conditional-expectation pass and checked their use here. Key `durrett2019`.
4. He Wang, [*Foundations of Probability and Statistical Theory*](https://wanghemath.github.io/Book-ProbabilityStatisticalTheory/chapters/chapter-20-bayesian-inference.html), Chapter 20 §24.1. Read its conditional-posterior construction for the posterior-mean pass. Key `wang2025_foundations`.
5. Nelder and Wedderburn, [“Generalized Linear Models” (1972)](https://www.maths.usyd.edu.au/u/jchan/GLM/Nelder1972GLM.pdf), §§1.1–1.3, pp. 371–373. Read its Bernoulli and Poisson response examples for the linear-predictor pass. Key `nelder_wedderburn1972`.
6. Chang and Pollard, [“Conditioning as disintegration” (1997)](https://www.stat.yale.edu/~jtc5/papers/ConditioningAsDisintegration.pdf), Example 11, checked the joint/marginal density ratio. Used as corroboration, without a bibliography entry.

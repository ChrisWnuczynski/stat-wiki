# M/M/1 queue

Type: concept. Rank 302. Tier: lighter.

## Facts

- **Checked by code:** For $\lambda=3,\mu=5$, $\rho=3/5$, $\pi_0=2/5$, $L=3/2$, $W=1/2$, and $\Pr(N\ge 2)=9/25$. For $\lambda=1,\mu=4$, $\pi_0=3/4$, $L=W=1/3$. For $\lambda=2,\mu=3$, $\pi_0=1/3$, $\Pr(N\ge 3)=8/27$, $L=2$, $W=1$. The M/M/∞ mean is $3$ and the empty probability is $e^{-3}$.
- **Checked:** The geometric series and $\sum n\rho^{n-1}=1/(1-\rho)^2$ are used in the proof.
- **Assumed:** Arrivals are Poisson and service is exponential, so the count is a birth–death chain with constant rates $\lambda$ and $\mu$.
- **Disputed:** None. Ross, Kelly, and Kendall were not opened; the geometric solution is derived from the cut balance.

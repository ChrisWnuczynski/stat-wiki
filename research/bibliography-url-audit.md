# Bibliography URL audit

Audited all 112 `url` fields in the 425-work bibliography on 2026-11-16. The URLs lead to publisher or journal pages, DOI-independent metadata catalogues (Open Library and OpenAlex), government and standards bodies, public-domain archives, author or institutional teaching pages, or official project and proceedings sites. The single URL for `fox2016_regression` that led to a third-party copy of the complete SAGE book was replaced with [SAGE's book page](https://www.sagepub.com/shop/buy-a-book/applied-regression-analysis-and-generalized-linear-models-3-237254). Its verification ID now matches that publisher URL.

No bibliography URL now points to an apparent unauthorized full-book mirror. The 1945 Fisher and Gauss links are historical public-domain scans; the Grinstead–Snell, Hoff, Geyer, and Statistics Canada PDFs are hosted by their authors, institutions, or issuing body. In research notes, the old Fox links were replaced by the publisher page. Other third-party PDF links in those notes for Rao, Raiffa–Schlaifer, and Box–Muller were replaced by DOI or publisher pages so the research trail does not direct readers to uncertain copies.

The offline citation schema check passed for all 425 works. This audit concerns link provenance; it does not claim that every external site will stay online or that a publisher page provides full-text access.

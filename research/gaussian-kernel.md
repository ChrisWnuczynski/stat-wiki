# Gaussian kernel research notes

- **Checked:** Wolfgang Polonik, “Nonparametric Inference – Kernel Density Estimation,” official LibreTexts full module https://stats.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Computing_and_Modeling/Supplemental_Modules_(Computing_and_Modeling)/Regression_Analysis/Nonparametric_Inference_-_Kernel_Density_Estimation . Read Introduction and definition, Oversmoothing and undersmoothing, and bandwidth discussion. Supports normalized Gaussian density kernel, whole-line support, and bandwidth trade-off.
- **Checked:** Rasmussen and Williams (2006), *Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning*, author/publisher-hosted full Ch. 4 PDF https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW4.pdf , §§4.1–4.2.1 pp. 79–84. Supports RBF/squared-exponential kernel, Gram positive semidefiniteness, length scale and smoothness. Existing bibliography key.
- **Checked:** Publisher preview of HTF, *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, Ch. 6 pp. 191–192 https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-0-387-84858-7_6 . Its introductory distinction between localization and inner-product kernels matches the page, but the preview does not cover the full chapter and is not cited for detailed formulas.
- **Checked:** Hansen and Larsson, *Computational Statistics with R*, full author-hosted Ch. 2 §2.1 https://cswr.nrhstat.org/density.html . The chapter explains KDE and its Gaussian kernel and explicitly attributes the Parzen–Rosenblatt window name to Parzen (1962) and Rosenblatt (1956). This full treatment supports the reader-facing history claim.
- **Checked:** Stanford Statistics official technical-report metadata for Parzen’s 1961 preprint https://statistics.stanford.edu/technical-reports/estimation-probability-density-function-and-mode , and the publisher DOI metadata of the 1962 article. The full original text was unavailable from the Stanford repository; metadata is retained only in these notes, not as a reader-facing citation.

# Independent random variables

Rank 362. Type: concept/definition. Tier: lighter.

## Checked
- Pishro-Nik §5.1: discrete X and Y are independent when the joint mass function equals the product of the marginals at every pair, and the conditional mass function then equals the marginal.
- The Bernoulli product 0.2, the dependent table with means 0.5 and 0.7 and cross moment 0.4, and the coin calculation 1/4 were recomputed.

## Assumed
- Expectations in the product identity exist. The finite-support proof does not cover every pair of variables with infinite support.

## Disputed
- None. The table is a counter-example to reading a product of means as independence.

## Sources read
- Hossein Pishro-Nik, *Introduction to Probability, Statistics, and Random Processes*, §5.1.

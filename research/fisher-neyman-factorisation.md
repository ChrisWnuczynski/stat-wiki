# Fisher–Neyman factorisation

Type: Theorem. Rank 283. Tier: lighter.

## Facts

- **Checked:** For a discrete mass function \(p(x\mid\theta)=g_\theta(T(x))h(x)\), the conditional probability of \(x\) given \(T=t\) equals \(h(x)\) divided by the sum of \(h\) over the same level set. The parameter cancels.
- **Checked by code:** Four Bernoulli trials with two successes have \(\binom{4}{2}=6\) sequences, each with conditional probability \(1/6\). Three trials with two successes give \(1/3\). Five trials with one success give \(1/5\).
- **Assumed:** A common dominating measure so that a joint mass or density exists. The worked cases are discrete with finite level sets.
- **Disputed:** Nothing numerical. The dominated-family converse was not proved. Fisher (1922) and the measure-theoretic construction were not opened.
